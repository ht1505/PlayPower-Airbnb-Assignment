## 5. Lighting & Atmosphere Standards

- **Interior Color Temperature:** Strictly warm white (2700K – 3000K). Soft illumination from recessed ceiling downlights, linen-shaded sconces, and pendant lamps.
- **Daylight Quality:** Bright, warm, diffuse tropical sunlight. Soft golden-hour rays or clear morning sun streaming through sheer curtains and casting gentle architectural shadows.
- **Exterior Lighting (Dusk/Evening shots):** Warm exterior facade uplighting (3000K), glowing jacuzzi underwater LED lights, warm terrace downlights, illuminated pool under deep cobalt twilight sky.
- **NO Harsh Digital Lighting:** Avoid flat camera-mounted flash, overblown HDR halos, or cold blue clinical interior lights.

## 6. Photography & Camera Guidelines

- **Style Reference:** High-end architectural and hospitality publications (*Architectural Digest*, *Condé Nast Traveler*, *The Local Project*).
- **Perspective & Lines:** 
  - Strict two-point perspective for room overviews.
  - Perfectly vertical verticals (no tilt distortion, no wide-angle keystoning, no fisheye curved doorframes).
  - Camera height: Standard architectural eye/waist level (approx 1.1m – 1.4m off finished floor).
- **Focal Lengths:**
  - Room overviews / establishing shots: 24mm – 35mm equivalent full-frame.
  - Vignettes & furniture groupings: 45mm – 55mm equivalent.
  - Material close-ups & styling details: 70mm – 90mm macro/portrait equivalent with natural shallow depth-of-field.
- **Compositional Balance:** Leading lines drawing towards natural light sources (balconies, windows, terraces), balanced negative space, clean uncluttered styling.
